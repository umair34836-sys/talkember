GPT-6 is coming to free ChatGPT users: what "Intelligent UI" is and what changes for you

By Your Name · 8 Oct 2026 · Updated 9 Oct 2026 · 3 min read

In one minute

OpenAI is rolling out GPT-6 to every ChatGPT tier, including Free and Go. The headline feature, Intelligent UI, lets answers come with charts, maps, buttons and small tools instead of just text. Here is what is new and when you get it.

  3. What "Intelligent UI" actually means

Key takeaways

  • OpenAI announced on 7 October 2026 that GPT-6 is coming to all ChatGPT plans, not just paid ones.
  • Plus, Pro, Business and Enterprise users get it first; Free and Go users get it from the next day.
  • Paid plans run on GPT-6 Sol; Free and Go run on GPT-6 Luna.
  • The big new feature is Intelligent UI: answers can include charts, maps, buttons, forms and small interactive tools.
  • ChatGPT can now start answering while it is still thinking, so long answers feel faster.

Who gets GPT-6, and when

Plan Model Rollout
Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise GPT-6 Sol From 7 October 2026
Free, Go GPT-6 Luna From 8 October 2026

Rollout is global but gradual, so it may take some time to appear on your account. For Enterprise users, availability depends on what the workplace admin allows. The update covers the normal Chat experience; OpenAI says the models behind its Work and Codex products are not changing in this release.

OpenAI first released GPT-6 models to paying customers in September. This release is about bringing that generation to the more than 1.2 billion people OpenAI says use ChatGPT every week.

What "Intelligent UI" actually means

Until now, ChatGPT mostly replied with text, tables and the occasional image. With Intelligent UI, GPT-6 decides what shape the answer should take:

  • Comparisons can appear side by side.
  • Plans can be laid out visually — for example, a cooking timeline next to the recipe, or road-trip stops on a map.
  • Explanations can become interactive diagrams where you change a number and see what happens.
  • Mini tools on request — ask for a savings calculator, a bill splitter for dinner with friends, or a simple game, and it builds one inside the chat.

When plain text is the best answer, it is supposed to stick to plain text.

Behind the scenes, OpenAI built a library of ready-made interface pieces and a system that shows the interface piece by piece as it is generated, so you don't wait for the full answer before seeing anything.

Faster answers to harder questions

Reasoning models used to make you wait until all the "thinking" was done. GPT-6 can think and answer at the same time, adding useful parts of the answer as it goes.

OpenAI's own figures:

  • For questions that need a web search, GPT-6 Instant starts answering 44% sooner on average than GPT-5.6 Instant.
  • It is better at deciding when to look something up and at finding information that supports its answer.

These are OpenAI's internal tests, not independent benchmarks.

Safety changes

OpenAI says GPT-6 is harder to trick into bypassing its safety rules — especially through attacks spread over several messages — while refusing fewer harmless requests. It also says the model is better at admitting when it lacks the information or tools to answer. Details are in the published system card linked below.

What you should do

  • Free users: nothing to install. Update the ChatGPT app and watch for the new responses over the coming days.
  • Students and professionals: interactive answers are great for learning, but still double-check facts, numbers and citations before you rely on them.
  • Privacy: be careful about pasting personal or company data into any chatbot.
