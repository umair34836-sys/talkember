Privacy policy

This policy explains what information FireCraft (“we”) collects when you visit https://firecraft.fun, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Comments: comments are powered by giscus and stored in GitHub Discussions. When you comment you sign in with GitHub, and GitHub's privacy statement applies. We don't receive your GitHub password.
  • Usage data: basic, anonymous counts of page views to see which posts are useful.
  • Emails you send us: used only to reply to you.

Cookies

Your theme and text-size choices are saved in your own browser's local storage and never sent to us.

Advertising (Google AdSense)

We show ads served by Google AdSense to keep this site free.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors from the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland are shown a consent message (provided through Google's certified consent tools) before personalised ads are used.

Analytics

If enabled, we use Google Analytics to understand overall traffic. It uses cookies and collects data such as pages visited and device type. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

How long we keep data

Comments stay published until you ask us to remove them or we delete them during moderation. Emails are kept only as long as needed to handle your request.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete any personal information we hold about you, including your comments. Email contact@firecraft.fun.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their information.
