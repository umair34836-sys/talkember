Welcome — what this site is and how we work

By Your Name · 8 Oct 2026 · Updated 8 Oct 2026 · 1 min read

In one minute

A quick tour: what we publish, how we check facts, how we use AI, and how you can join the conversation.

On this page

  1. Key takeaways
  2. What you'll find here
  3. How we check facts
  4. How we use AI
  5. Join the conversation

Key takeaways

  • We publish daily news, explainers, how-to guides, videos and open discussions.
  • Every post shows its sources, a fact-check label and when it was last updated.
  • If AI helped draft a post, we say so on the post itself.
  • You can comment on any post. Comments are reviewed before they appear.

What you'll find here

Short, clear posts in Hindi and English. Each one starts with a summary so you can get the point in under a minute, and goes deeper for anyone who wants the details.

How we check facts

Every post carries one of four labels — Fact-checked, Developing story, Opinion or Not yet verified — and lists the sources it relies on at the bottom. When we get something wrong we fix it and record the change in our public corrections log.

How we use AI

AI sometimes helps us produce a first draft. A human editor always reviews, checks and edits before anything is published, and those posts are clearly marked as AI-assisted.
