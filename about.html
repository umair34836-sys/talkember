About us

TalkEmber is an independent site publishing daily news, explainers, how-to guides, videos and open discussions in Hindi and English.

Why we started

Too much of the internet is slow, stuffed with pop-ups, and hard to trust. We wanted a place where you get the point quickly, can see where every fact came from, and can talk back.

What makes us different

  • A one-minute summary at the top of every post.
  • Sources and a fact-check label on every post.
  • Original, verified content — we publish what we have checked ourselves.
  • A public corrections log — we own our mistakes.
  • Limited ads that never cover the content or make the page jump.
  • Moderated comments so conversations stay useful and respectful.
