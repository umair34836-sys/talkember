Editorial & AI policy

How we decide what to publish, how we check it, and how we use AI.

Our standards

  • Answer the reader's real question first, then explain.
  • Separate news from opinion. Opinion pieces are labelled “Opinion”.
  • No clickbait: the headline must match what the post actually says.

Fact-check labels

  • Fact-checked — claims checked against the sources listed on the post.
  • Developing story — confirmed so far, but details may change.
  • Opinion — the author's view and experience.
  • Not yet verified — published early; treat with caution until sources are added.

How we use AI

We sometimes use AI tools to help produce a first draft, outline or summary. Every AI-assisted post is reviewed, fact-checked and edited by a human editor before publishing, and is labelled “AI-assisted · human-reviewed” at the top. AI is never used to invent quotes, sources, data or eyewitness accounts.

Corrections

If we get something wrong we fix it quickly, add a note to the post, and list it on our public corrections page. Report an error to contact@talkember.site.

Independence

Advertisers do not influence what we write. Sponsored content, if any, is clearly labelled.
